## Label Maker 0.1.10.0 (classical music, audiobooks and podcasts wait for you)

### What is new
- **Classical music, audiobooks and podcasts are recognized and never filed automatically.** These are the albums where a confident-looking match is most likely to be wrong: classical music is named by work and movement rather than by song, and audiobooks and podcasts are hardly on MusicBrainz at all. Label Maker now notices them and sends them to the review list with a plain sentence, for example: "This looks like classical music (the genre tag says "Classical"), which is named by work and movement rather than by song, so it needs your eye." Your own choices on the Albums tab work as always (approve, pick another edition, skip, discard). Rules: `docs/MATCHING-RULES.md` section 8i.
  - **Podcast:** the genre tag or the album name says so.
  - **Audiobook:** the genre tag says audiobook, spoken word, speech and the like; or one file is longer than 90 minutes; or at least three files have titles that start like chapters.
  - **Classical:** the genre tag says classical, opera, baroque, orchestral and the like; or at least two files have titles that name works and movements ("Symphony", "Concerto", "Sonata", "Op. 67", "Allegro", a Roman numeral).
  - The composer tag alone is not enough, and one odd file among many ordinary ones changes nothing.
- Label Maker reads the genre and composer tags for this. It never writes them, and nothing new is sent anywhere.
- Nothing else changed: thresholds, the edition rule, cover art, duplicate choices and the song-title search work as before. The settings page is the same as in 0.1.9.0.

### Still true
This is a trial build that **starts in dry run**. Install and first steps: `docs/INSTALL-TRIAL.md`.

### How it was tested
353 unit tests (twelve new: each rule, the cases that must not be flagged, reading the genre and composer without ever writing them, and a certain match tagged "Classical" going to review with nothing moved); the live test (62 checks) on a private, throw-away Emby 4.10 instance. On the 322 albums of your library exports one was recognized (a kids' collection with a file longer than 90 minutes); the exports had no genre tags, so the genre rules could not be measured. **Not yet seen:** these on your own server.

### Known limits
A long DJ mix is also held for review (a file over 90 minutes). A single song that is on many albums often cannot be matched to the right one by its title alone. No cover art embedded in the tags.

### Verify the download
`LabelMaker.dll.sha256` has the SHA-256. The file also carries a signed build attestation: `gh attestation verify LabelMaker.dll --repo feerlessleadr/label-maker-emby`
